# Product

## Register

product

## Users

sAiity is for people who want fast, private voice dictation in any iPhone text
field. They use the keyboard in real conversations and work, often switching
between languages and needing a clear indication that recording and processing
are active.

## Product Purpose

sAiity turns a press-and-hold gesture into polished, on-device text. The app
manages local recognition models and preferences; the keyboard stays focused on
recording, status, and insertion. Success means the keyboard feels native,
model choices remain understandable, and the app never hides or invents the
state of a download or transcription.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and native. The product should feel like a considered Apple
utility: calm during waiting, direct during actions, and restrained in its use
of color and motion.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic glassmorphism, decorative gradients, oversized branded headers,
dashboard-style card grids, and controls that expose implementation details
instead of user-facing choices. Do not copy the source project's visual
identity or leave legacy keyboard chrome visible when the voice-only surface is
active.

## Design Principles

- Keep the voice gesture and its state immediately legible.
- Prefer native iOS navigation, controls, materials, and semantic colors.
- Show complete model information and truthful lifecycle states in context.
- Keep the keyboard visually quiet so the active text field remains primary.
- Preserve accessibility and readability when transparency or motion is reduced.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Dynamic Type, semantic system colors, explicit labels for icon-only
controls, and readable fallbacks when transparency is reduced. Dictation must
remain understandable through text and status changes, not color or animation
alone.
