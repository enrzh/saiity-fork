## Measurement and decision

Run the consented corpus both raw and with cleanup. Keep failed, cancelled and
timed-out cases; record actual raw/final text, language/script preservation,
protected facts and a human rating where one was given. Separate ordinary
recognition errors from cleanup changes. Inspect silence hallucinations.

Measure cold preparation and warm Stop-to-proxy p50/p95 separately, including
ASR, cleanup and handoff stages. Use at least 20 inserted cases per timing group
before interpreting p95. In-app copy/share has no keyboard proxy: record its
delivery endpoint separately rather than inventing a Stop-to-proxy value.
Record peak memory, cancellation/foreground-loss latency, repeated-run thermal
state and idle/background battery observations on the phone. Simulator figures
do not substitute for these measurements.
